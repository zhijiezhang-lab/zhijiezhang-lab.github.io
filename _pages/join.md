Students and postdocs interested in integrating models and experiments to understand the origin and maintenance of biodiversity are encouraged to contact us. Please email zhijie.zhang at pku.edu.cn, outlining your research background, motivation, and potential project ideas, and attaching your CV.

## Postdocs
We are looking for a postdoc with theoretical ecology background to study the coexistence of large number of species, using the Consumer-Resource and Lotka-Volterra competition models. However, candidates with a relevant background and strong publication record should email me as there are several internal and external funding sources. 

## Ph.D students
We admit only one Ph.D. student per year, so early contact is essential. We recommend reaching out by your sixth semester if you are a bachelor's student, or your fifth semester if you are a master's student.

## Undergraduate and Research assistant
We also keep one or two undergraduates (or research assistants) working in the lab.
