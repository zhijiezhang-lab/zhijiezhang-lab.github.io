We are a team of researchers in the Institute of Ecology at Peking University, led by Zhijie Zhang. We work at the intersection of ecology and evolution to study the fundamental processes that govern the origin and maintenance of biodiversity. By integrating theoretical models with experimental approaches, we explore the dynamic feedback between ecological context and evolutionary change, aiming to predict biodiversity patterns. Ultimately, our goal is to build a framework that guides strategies for ecosystem stewardship in a changing world.
